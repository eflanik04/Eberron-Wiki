Arcanix


A small farming village in the nation of Aundair, which also happens to be an institute of fine arcane studies.

Floating Towers

The magical university of Arcanix exists in the floating towers above the city. This University is the most prestigious school of magical learning, and is home to the most powerful mages in Khorvaire.

Much like the towers of Sharn, the university levitates high above the village, with much of their interior devoted to student dorms, laboratories, libraries, and classrooms.

Generally the Towers tend to be lit by everburning torches, though these can be darkened. The towers have a strong aura of transmutation about them as a result of 800 years worth of magical tinkering. Among these changes includes mild temperatures in all of the rooms, manifest zones to other planes, and extradimensional spaces not confined by the size of their exteriors.

Known Towers

  • Glarehold: One of the two towers devoted to training future spellcasters, including student residences. It is also home to the White Arch, where new students will often enter the academy for the first time.
  • Amberwall: The second of the towers dedicated to students. In its lowest depths, the Maze of Shadowy Terror teaches students humility by testing their powers.
  • Nocturnas: This is one of two towers that serves as a home and personal study for the members of the Arcane Congress, including their personal laboratories and libraries. They often live here while advising Aundair on matters related to magic and their rivalry with The Twelve. In its center is Adal's Demesne, which houses Adal ir'Wynarn, Minister of Magic. Near the top is the towers observatory and a series of menhirs known as the Gate of Xabra. Nocturnas is also the location of secret labs where Adal and his staff attempt to create powerful weapons.
  • Skyreach: Much like Nocturnas, Skyreach is home to members of the Arcane Congress. It is also the location of the Five Nations greatest store of arcane lore, the Library of Robideur.
  • Tower of Alchemy: The Tower of Alchemy is home to Tzandro Kavalant, a senior mentor who teaches pupils in the art of alchemy.

Arcane Congress

The Arcane Congress was established by Galifar ir'Wynarn I. in 15 YK. The congress was tasked with studying the mysteries of magic and to place these powers at the service of the Kingdom of Galifar. The members of the Congress oversee the towers of wizardry, advise Aurala ir'Wynarn, and train new wizards and magewrights.
