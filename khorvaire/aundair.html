Geography and Features

Topography

Breland is a mix of open farmland, woodland, and sprawling metropolises.

Borders

  • Zilargo to the east
  • Droaam to the West
  • Eldeen Reaches and Aundair to North
  • Darguun and the Mournland to the far east

Transportation

The lightning rail originates from Sharn, and travels out across Breland to access the other nations. Orien Trade Routes branch out to the more countryside.

Geographical Features

  • Faded Forest
  • King's Forest
  • Blackcaps
  • Graywall Mountains
  • Howling Peaks
  • Dagger River
  • The Hilt

Exclusive to Breland

The Hilt

The mouth of the Dagger River and a bay in the Thunder Sea. This is the main passageway to ports located in Sharn and Moonwatch.

Dagger River

A river originating in the north near Woodhelm, running south to The Hilt and eventually into the Thunder Sea.
