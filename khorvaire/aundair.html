Geography and Features

Topography

Aundair is mainly natural geographical features, apart from the cities. It is often sprawling grasslands and small forests. The climate is temperate and the soil is fertile, making it a haven for good farmland.

Borders

  • Thrane to the Southeast
  • Scions Sea to the East
  • Eldeen Bay to the North
  • Eldeen Reaches, Wynarn River, and Lake Galifar to the West
  • Breland and The Blackcaps to the South

Transportation

The lightning rail passes through the larger cities of Passage and Fairhaven. House Orien trade roads network through the smaller cities and westward to the Eldeen Reaches.

Aundair River

Ariver that flows through eastern Aundair from Fairhaven to Scions Sound.

The Crying Fields

A region east of Ghalt and near the border to Thrane. During The Last War, the Crying Fields were the site of five battles between Thrane and Aundair. These fields now display some sort of haunting or curse, as the grass that now grows here has become a bloody crimson red. The sounds of battle can be heard from out of the fog.

Duskwood

A forest in Aundair, standing on the shores of Lake Galifar and the Wynarn River.

Fairhold

The Royal Court of Fairhold is the palace of Queen Aurala ir'Wynarn.

Starpeak Mountains and Observatory

The Starpeak Mountains are located in east Aundair, and is the location of the Starpeak Observatory. The Observatory was built by King Daroon ir'Wynarn in 512 YK. Its purpose is to chart the moons and stars in the hopes of providing signs of portents for the future. The Arcane Congress now uses the site to study the moons and stars.

Whisper Rock and Whisper Woods

Whisper Rock is a mysterious stone ruin deep in the Whisper Woods of northern Aundair. The crumbling rock monolith has stood since the early days of Galifar, and is possibly as old as the Dhakaani Empire. It is believed to be the source of the mysterious murmuring in the woods around it.

History

In -2500, the humans who had spread over Khorvaire formed five distinct settlements, the Five Nations. Aundair, first established as just Thaliost, would expand south and west from the area around the city, and eventually become Aundair. In -1800, the Mark of Handling appeared among the humans of Thaliost.

During The Last War, Aundair spent most of its time in warfare against either Thrane or Karrnath, eventually losing Thaliost to Thrane. During the War, the Eldeen Reaches seceded from Aundair, causing the nation to lose half of its total area, along with a fifth of the population.

Civilization

Aundair is theoretically an absolute monarchy, currently ruled by Aurala ir'Wynarn. However, in practice, it is governed by and unofficial triumvirate consisting of Queen Aurala ir'Wynarn, Prince Adal ir'Wynarn, and Lord Darro ir'Lain. None of the three entirely trust the other two, and as such they have a gentle rivalry.

Aundair's people are a model of duality. They consist of hardworking agriculturalists and learned individuals. Metropolises are bustling with activity as two major dragonmarked house enclaves and two centers of learning draw heavy business. Solitary monasteries dot the landscape where monks live in complete solitude.

Aundairian wine is a staple of the nation and is a well known export. Two great centers of learning reside in Aundair, the University of Wynarn and the floating towers of Arcanix.

The majority worship The Sovereign Host, however there are some faithful to the Church of the Silver Flame, calling themselves Puritans.

Fairhaven

The capital city of Aundair. It is the largest city in the nation and is home to Queen Aurala ir'Wynarn and her royal family. The city is a major commerical and cultural center for Aundair. It is divided into twelve wards by the city planning district in an effort to better manage the resources of the city.

Ghalt

A small town in central Aundair. It lies near the lightning rail, but does not have a stop. Its primary industry is in vineyards.

Passage

The second largest city in Aundair. Passage is a trade and commerce location, that features a large House Orien enclave named Journey's Home. Passage also features a large lightning rail maintenance and repair facility.

Stormhome

An island city at the north edge of the Scions Sound that serves as the seat of power for House Lyrandar. The mages of Lyrandar use magic to maintain a temperate climate on the island. In 347 YK, House Lyrandar petitioned the crown of Galifar to set up a house enclave in Stormhome. Over the next three years, Lyrandar used magical means to divert storms from the island, turning it into a tropical paradise. The island is also a port, and generates a decent amount of tourism since The Last War. House Ghallanda has constructed a fair amount of resorts on the island.

Wyr

a village in western Aundair near the border of the Eldeen Reaches. The warrior monks of the Monastery of Orla-un are located on the edge of the village.

Dust Motes

A faction of the Lords of Dust based in Passage. It is a hub operation sending out agents into the rest of Khorvaire to sow seeds of discontent. A current scheme is being lead by a Rakasha named Kkressd who has taken the form of a House Orien scion and is courting Baron Jorlanna d'Cannith to split House Cannith.

Knights Arcane

A specialized military group headed by Lord Darro ir'Lain, who are loyal to the crown. They are Aundairs strongest and most capable units.

Aundairian Royal Family

Aundairs royal family descends from the lines of Galifar through King Wrogar, the fourth child of the last king of Galifar. Its current royal family consists of the now deceased matriarch, Queen Barvette. Her eldest child, Aurala ir'Wynarn, now rules Aundair. Her four younger siblings include her two brothers prince Adal ir'Wynarn, and Prince Aurad, as well as her two sisters Princess Wrel and Princess Wrey, who study magic at Arcanix. Queen Auralas current consort is Sasik d'Vadalis, and they have three children together. Crown Prince Wrogar and Princess Corrine live with them in Fairhold, while Prince Jurian resides in Wroat, some believe in self imposed exile.

Mosaic Committee

A secret society nestled within the Arcane Congress floating above Arcanix. This sect of wizards believe that the fulfillment of the Draconic Prophecy will result in the destruction of the world. They feel that the only way to save the world is to destroy pieces of the prophecy as they are discovered.
